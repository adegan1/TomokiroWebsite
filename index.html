Who Am I?

Hi! I'm Andrew Degan, a passionate designer, developer, and creator based in Aptos, California. I specialize in Video Game design and production, built upon my love for turning humble ideas into immersive experiences, especially those that bring joy and inspiration to others.

My Story

I have been uniquely interested in the medium of Video Games for as long as I can remember. Each game I played sparked a desire to understand how it was made on a technical level, leading me to explore the vast world of game development. From design, programming, and art to storytelling and understanding how people interact with video games from a psychological perspective. I strive to create the best experiences possible, and I am always eager to learn and grow as a creator!

From a young age, I have participated in programming classes and various game development projects both solo and collaboratively, honing my skills across a wide range of disciplines. From my work in my high school's career center to my study abroad in Japan where I focused on cultural differences in game design and player experience, I have had the wonderful opportunity to work on a variety of projects ranging from small game jams to larger scale productions at my University. I value personalization, and thus this very website has been built from the ground up by me, hand-coded in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with accessibility and creativity in mind!
